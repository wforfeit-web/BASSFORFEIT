BASSFORFEIT

A mono bass synth with a tempo-synced wobble

140 BPM

No sound? Turn up the volume, or switch off silent mode on older iPhones.

Tap a step to place F1. Play a key to change the note. Tap the same note again to remove it. Neighbouring steps slide together.

Keyboard

Octave 1
Drag knobs up or down, or focus one and use the arrow keys. Double-tap a knob to reset it. On a computer, play with A–L and W–P, Z and X change octave, and Space starts the pattern.
